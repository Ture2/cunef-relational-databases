The rules in one sentence
1NF
One cell, one value. No lists or repeating groups in a cell or a row.
2NF
The whole key. Every attribute depends on the entire key, not on part of it. It only matters with composite keys.
3NF
Nothing but the key. No attribute depends on another attribute that is not a key.
BCNF
Every determinant is a key. If something determines other attributes, it must identify each row.
4NF
One fact per table. Two independent pieces of data about the same thing do not share a table.
5NF
Nothing that can be rebuilt from its parts. If a table comes from joining smaller ones, store it as those parts.
